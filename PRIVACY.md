# Privacy Policy — Calibrate

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Calibrate does not use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. It does use Apple services to provide synchronization, leaderboards, friend groups, and subscriptions.

## Data stored on your device

Your questions, answers, confidence intervals, scores, streaks, settings, and subscription-access state are stored locally using Apple frameworks.

## Data stored in iCloud

When iCloud is available, Calibrate sends answer history, scores, streaks, and profile information to the app's CloudKit container. Answer history and profile data are stored in the signed-in user's private CloudKit database so they can sync across that user's devices.

If you participate in the global leaderboard or a friend group, your chosen display name, calibration score, number of answers, premium badge state, and a CloudKit-generated account identifier are stored in the app's public CloudKit database. Other Calibrate users may see the display name and leaderboard information you publish. Do not use a display name that reveals information you do not want to make public.

Apple processes CloudKit and StoreKit data under Apple's own privacy terms. Subscription purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit; Calibrate receives verified entitlement and transaction information but does not receive your payment-card details.

## Notifications

Daily reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device. Calibrate does not operate a remote push-notification server.

## Data sharing and tracking

Calibrate does not sell personal data and does not use data for advertising or tracking. Public leaderboard and friend-group data is shared only to provide those features.

## Retention and deletion

Local app data can be removed by deleting the app. iCloud data may remain in Apple's CloudKit service according to the user's iCloud settings and Apple's retention behavior. For assistance with app-managed CloudKit data, open a support request at the contact link below.
